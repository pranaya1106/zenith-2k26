## Brand Elements

### Foreground Elements

#### 1. **fg-gemini**
- Pink/magenta 3D curved shape
- Organic, flowing design
- Associated with Google Gemini AI

#### 2. **fg-openai**
- White/silver interwoven knot pattern
- Geometric, symmetrical design
- Associated with OpenAI

#### 3. **fg-claude**
- Orange asterisk/star burst shape
- 3D appearance with depth
- Associated with Anthropic Claude AI

### Background Elements

#### **bg-elements-top**
- Pink/magenta organic wave shapes
- Positioned at top of layouts
- Gradient from dark red to bright pink
- Creates dynamic header interest

#### **bg-elements-left**
- Purple/blue organic flowing shape
- Positioned on left side
- Creates depth and visual interest
- Curved, ribbon-like design

#### **bg-elements-right**
- Green/teal layered organic shapes
- Positioned on right side
- Stacked flowing ribbons
- Vibrant gradient effect

### Decorative Shapes

#### **shapes-v1**
- Purple rounded rectangle with curved indent on right side
- Modern, minimal design

#### **shapes-v2**
- Large purple gradient background with organic flowing patterns
- White curved accent in bottom right
- Smooth, wave-like transitions
- Full-bleed background design

#### **shapes-v3**
- Cyan/turquoise quatrefoil or clover shape
- Symmetrical four-lobed design

#### **shapes-v4**
- Pink/coral rounded rectangle with curved top indent
- Soft, approachable aesthetic

#### **bg-bottom**
- Large organic wave pattern
- Purple/blue gradient
- Flows across bottom of layouts
- Creates dynamic visual interest

### Effects

#### **left-lighting-effect**
- Subtle gradient lighting from left side
- Creates depth and atmosphere
- Dark to light transition

#### **right-lighting-effect**
- Subtle gradient lighting from right side
- Mirrors left effect for balance
- Adds dimensional quality

### Patterns

#### **bg-grid-pattern**
- Subtle grid overlay
- Cross/plus symbol pattern
- Low opacity for background use
- Adds technical/modern feel

### Textures

#### **bg-texture**
- Dark textured background
- Subtle noise/grain effect
- Adds depth without distraction
- Professional finish

## Design Principles

### Color Usage
- Dark backgrounds (black/charcoal) as primary surface
- Purple accent colors for hierarchy and emphasis
- High contrast white/black logos for versatility
- Vibrant accent colors (cyan, pink, orange) for AI brand elements

### Typography Guidelines
- Plus Jakarta Sans for all text
- Clear hierarchy from Normal to Extra Bold
- Size ranges provide flexibility
- Modern, geometric sans-serif aesthetic

### Visual Style
- Modern, tech-forward aesthetic
- 3D elements and depth effects
- Organic shapes contrasted with geometric patterns
- Professional yet approachable design language

### Layout Principles
- Dark-themed design system
- Layered backgrounds with textures and patterns
- Lighting effects for depth
- Grid-based structure with organic accents

## Asset Naming Convention

### Logos
- Format: `[brand-name]-[color]-[background]`
- Examples: `scope-club-color-dark`, `aws-white-dark`, `zenith-2k26-color-light`

### Elements
- Foreground: `fg-[name]`
- Shapes: `shapes-v[number]`
- Backgrounds: `bg-[name]`
- Effects: `[position]-lighting-effect`
- Patterns: `bg-[type]-pattern`
- Textures: `bg-texture`

## Technical Specifications

### File Formats (Recommended)
- **Logos**: SVG (vector), PNG (raster backup)
- **Shapes**: SVG for scalability
- **Textures**: PNG or WebP
- **Patterns**: SVG or CSS

### Color Format
- **Digital**: Hex codes
- **Print**: Convert to CMYK as needed

### Minimum Sizes
- **Logo minimum width**: 120px (digital), 1 inch (print)
- **Body text minimum**: 14px
- **Caption minimum**: 12px
